Bin Bypass app preview

Use it like a collector (find units with containers ready, plan a run, cash in) or switch to CDS / Council at the top to see fleet health, live reporting and data downloads.

Dummy data. The units in Hobart and Brisbane are made-up test units for testing the server and the app at scale. They are not real collections.

Open the app full screen ↗ · on a phone, use “Add to Home Screen” there to keep it.

Things to try

  1. Tap 📍 on the map and set your suburb. Value counts only units within 50 km.
  2. Open a unit to see its six slots and history.
  3. Plan a route on the Walk tab and end it at a refund point.
  4. Switch to CDS / Council and download data by city, suburb or unit.
